# Kindling

**For the late-identified adult who finally got the answer.**

Kindling is a neuro-affirming AI coach for adults who found out about ADHD (or AuDHD) later in life. It does not try to make the brain normal. It helps you notice your wiring, design a life that fits, and practise one small experiment at a time.

- Capacity-first check-in before anything else
- Scripted coaching loop: what matters → what’s in the way → one experiment
- Session notes you can keep and re-read
- Skills cards: time blindness, demand avoidance, RSD, transitions, masking, interest-based attention
- Pattern memory over weeks
- Restart protocol — drift is expected, shame is not a feature
- Late-diagnosis arc woven through every screen
- Kokoro on-device voice (Heart), with Piper and system-voice fallbacks
- Local-only PWA. Nothing leaves the phone unless you export.

Not therapy. Not a diagnosis. Not medication advice. Not crisis care.

Part of the Kindling family — adults first, then teens, kids, older adults.
